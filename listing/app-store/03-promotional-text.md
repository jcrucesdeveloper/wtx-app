# App Store — Promotional Text

Limit: 170 characters. Sits right under the app name on the product page,
above the description. Unique to iOS — **not indexed for search**, but the
one field you can edit anytime without triggering a new App Review, so it's
the right place for anything time-sensitive (a launch message now, a seasonal
hook later) without waiting on a build.

**English (164/170 chars):**
```
Log every set in one tap, see your PRs and streak, and train live with friends. Routines are plain text you can share with a link or QR. No account needed to start.
```

**Spanish (167/170 chars):**
```
Registra cada serie con un toque, mira tus récords y tu racha, y entrena en vivo con amigos. Tus rutinas son texto: compártelas con un enlace o QR. Empieza sin cuenta.
```

Both counts were checked with a script. Update this whenever there's real
news (a new feature ships, a seasonal push) — it's cheap to change and
doesn't require a new binary.
