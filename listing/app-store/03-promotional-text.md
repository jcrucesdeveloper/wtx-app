# App Store — Promotional Text

Limit: 170 characters. Sits right under the app name on the product page,
above the description. Unique to iOS — **not indexed for search**, but the
one field you can edit anytime without triggering a new App Review, so it's
the right place for anything time-sensitive (a launch message now, a seasonal
hook later) without waiting on a build.

**Primary recommendation (103/170 chars):**
```
Plan workout routines as plain text - no account, no cloud. Share any routine instantly with a QR code.
```

**Alternative (101/170 chars):**
```
No account, no cloud - your workout routines stay on your phone as plain text. Share one with a scan.
```

Both counts verified programmatically. Update this whenever there's real
news (a new feature ships, a review roundup, a seasonal push) — it's cheap
to change and doesn't require a new binary.
