# Google Play — Graphics Checklist

Specs: `research/04-asset-specs.md`. Shot list/content plan: `assets/checklist.md`.

- [ ] (Optional) Tablet screenshots, same format rules
- [ ] (Optional) Short promo video URL (YouTube) — not required, skip unless
      one already exists; don't produce one just for this submission
